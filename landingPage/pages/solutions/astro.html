Seamless Integration

FormBox works flawlessly with Astro. Because FormBox is completely headless, you have full control over your form's HTML and CSS.

<form action="https://api.formbox.com/submit/YOUR_FORM_ID" method="POST">
  <input type="email" name="email" required />
  <button type="submit">Send</button>
</form>
  • ⚡ Plain HTML Support - Standard POST requests work out of the box.
  • ⚡ JSON Replies - Perfect for headless frontends making AJAX/Fetch requests.
  • ⚡ Redirects - Easily redirect users to a custom success page after submission.

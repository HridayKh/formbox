Why FormBox beats Getform

1. Unmatched Spam Protection

While Getform relies on basic reCAPTCHA implementations, FormBox gives you a multi-layered defense system out of the box. We include invisible Honeypot fields, rate limiting per endpoint, Altcha (a privacy-first, cookie-less proof-of-work CAPTCHA), and Cloudflare Turnstile BYOK. You block bots without degrading the user experience.

2. See Exactly When Emails Land

The most common question form owners get is "Did my submission go through?" FormBox is the only backend that provides native delivery monitoring. Right in your dashboard, you can see if a notification or autoresponder was Sent, Delivered, or Bounced. No more guessing if Getform's email servers are lagging.

3. Built for Agencies

Managing multiple clients on Getform often means sharing passwords or upgrading to massive enterprise plans. FormBox is built around a native Account → Folder → Form hierarchy. You can generate secure, read-only dashboards for your clients so they can view their own submissions without accessing your billing details or other clients' data.

Migrating from Getform is Easy

Switching takes less than two minutes. FormBox acts as a drop-in replacement for your existing HTML forms. Keep your current HTML and CSS exactly as it is—just swap out the action URL.

<!-- Simply replace the Getform URL with your FormBox endpoint -->
<form action="https://api.formbox.com/submit/YOUR_FORM_ID" method="POST">
  <input type="email" name="email" required />
  <button type="submit">Send</button>
</form>

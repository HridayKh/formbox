What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store data that can be recalled by a web server in the domain that placed the cookie. We use them exclusively for essential site functionality.

Why No Cookie Consent Banner

We only use strictly necessary cookies required for the essential operation of our platform. Under the ePrivacy Directive (and GDPR), consent is not required for strictly necessary cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

Cloudflare Turnstile may set its own strictly necessary cookies for CAPTCHA purposes to ensure security and prevent abuse. These are also exempt from consent requirements.

What We Don't Use

We do NOT use any analytics (such as Google Analytics or Plausible), tracking pixels, advertising networks, or social media trackers.

Managing Cookies

You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging into your account or using FormBox securely.

Changes

We may update this policy periodically. We encourage you to check back from time to time for the latest information on our privacy practices.

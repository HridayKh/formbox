Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

⚠️ Legal Disclaimer: This document was NOT created by lawyers and has NOT been verified or reviewed by legal professionals. It is provided as-is for transparency purposes only. We recommend consulting a qualified legal professional for advice specific to your situation. Formbox's GDPR compliance efforts have not been independently verified or audited.

1. Introduction

Welcome to FormBox. This Privacy Policy outlines how FormBox ("we," "our," or "us"), a service operated by Hriday Kh based in India, collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at support@formbox.dev.

2. Our Roles: Data Controller and Data Processor

FormBox operates in two capacities regarding personal data:

  • Data Controller: For FormBox users (account owners). We determine the purposes and means of processing your account information.
  • Data Processor: For form submitters. When end-users submit data through a form hosted by a FormBox user, FormBox processes this data on behalf of the form owner, who acts as the Data Controller.

3. Data Collection

3.1 Data from FormBox Users (Account Owners)

We collect: Email address, hashed password (managed by Supabase), OAuth profiles (if logging in via Google/GitHub), subscription details, and verified email addresses.

3.2 Data from Form Submitters

We collect on behalf of our users: All submitted form fields, hashed IP addresses, country (via Cloudflare header), referrer, user agent (sent to Akismet for spam detection), and file uploads.

4. Data Infrastructure and Sub-processors

Our primary application and self-hosted infrastructure (Redis for caching, S3/Garage for file uploads and CSV exports) are hosted in the EU (Contabo/Netcup). We use the following sub-processors:

Sub-processor Purpose Location/Notes
Supabase Database & Auth AWS Tokyo, Japan (Has EU adequacy decision)
Polar Billing & Subscriptions Global
ZeptoMail/Zoho Email delivery India
Akismet/Automattic Spam detection US (Receives IP, user agent, referrer, content)
Sentry Error monitoring EU (ingest.de.sentry.io)
Cloudflare CDN, CAPTCHA, Rate limiting Global
Google & GitHub OAuth login Global
Discord Webhook notifications Configured by form owner

5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for the operation of the service:

  • sb_token (1 hour): Authentication token.
  • sb_refresh (7 days): Token to refresh your session.

These are strict session and security cookies (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict).

6. Data Retention Policies

FormBox enforces automated data deletion to minimize data footprint:

  • Form Submissions: Free tier (7 days), Starter tier (30 days), Pro tier (90 days). Data is permanently and automatically deleted thereafter.
  • CSV Exports: Automatically deleted after 7 days.
  • Account Data: Retained while the account is active.

7. Your Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR (Articles 15-22), you have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to the processing of your personal data.

For Account Owners: Contact us at support@formbox.dev to exercise these rights.

For Form Submitters: FormBox acts as a processor. Please contact the form owner (the Data Controller) directly to exercise your rights regarding data submitted to their forms.

8. Limitation of Liability

While we implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data. Formbox shall not be liable for any data loss, corruption, unauthorized access, or breach arising from circumstances beyond our reasonable control. Our total aggregate liability for any claims relating to data protection shall not exceed the fees paid by you in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, FormBox will notify the appropriate supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after having become aware of it, per GDPR Art. 33.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.
